## Interpretation so far
Cost = (#MBs entering unified sub-partition search) x (per-search SATD cost).
Divergent content pushes 58% of Phase2 MBs into unified search vs 8% on coherent content.
SubPartBudget = SliceMbCount/4 = 510/slice = 2040/frame; unified MBs/frame = 505 (~126/slice)
-> the budget NEVER BINDS on this content. It caps a pathological 100%-of-MBs case at 25%,
   but 25% of MBs at ~300us each is already ~60ms/frame of thread work. (VERIFY semantics.)

## SubPartBudget semantics (H264BaselineSliceEncoder.cs:1893-1896)
_subPartMbCount++ for EVERY Phase2 MB with variance>=64 (whether or not unified search runs);
over budget => rangeCapThisMb = min(4, cap). That is all it does. It bounds nothing else.
On div content: ~505 unified MBs/frame ~= 126/slice vs budget 510/slice -> never binds.
